01 / The workshop

Beyond Reproducing Face-to-Face Interaction

Extended Reality (XR) enables social interactions to be transformed rather than merely reproduced by decoupling physical behavior from virtual representations. Research on Transformed Social Interaction (TSI) has shown how such transformations can influence embodied experiences, social perception, and interpersonal dynamics. Emerging generative and multimodal AI offers new possibilities for context-sensitive, adaptive transformations, while raising important questions about their implementation, social consequences, and ethical boundaries. This workshop brings together researchers and practitioners across XR, embodiment, human–AI interaction, and computer-mediated communication to explore existing and emerging transformation possibilities, envision AI-assisted adaptive TSI, and address its research and ethical challenges. Together, we aim to advance a shared research agenda for the next generation of TSI, exploring how adaptive transformations reshape not only embodied experiences, but also social interaction and human relationships.
